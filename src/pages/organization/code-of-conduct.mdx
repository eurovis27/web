# Code of Conduct

EuroVis is an inclusive and harassment-free conference that welcomes all participants independently of sex, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, race, ethnicity, nationality, citizenship, religion or belief, age, disability, pregnancy, physical appearance, socio-economic background, political opinion, or any other personal characteristic. If local law at the EuroVis venue provides broader protection, it applies in addition. Respectful communication is vital for a healthy exchange of ideas and thoughts. EuroVis does not tolerate abuse or harassment in any form. Behaviors such as exclusion, intimidation, use of abusive or degrading language, stalking, harassing photography or recording, inappropriate physical contact, unwelcome sexual attention, and all other forms of harassment will face consequences.

Anyone who experiences, observes, or has knowledge of such behavior is requested to report the incident to the ombuds persons ombuds@eurovis.org immediately. The reports and all information will be kept confidential. Even if a public response is deemed necessary, the identities of victims and reporters will remain confidential unless those individuals consent otherwise.
