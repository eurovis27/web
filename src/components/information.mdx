In 2027, the conference will take place for the first time in Stuttgart, Germany.
Stuttgart is the capital of the state of Baden-Württemberg and a vibrant city in southern Germany, known as the birthplace of the car, in a region well known for its tradition of creative thinking and inventiveness.
It is renowned for its thriving economy, cultural offerings, and as a center of education and research.

EuroVis 2027 will be hosted by the University of Stuttgart and Stuttgart Technical University of Applied Sciences, in cooperation with Planetarium Stuttgart.

Stuttgart will be an especially exciting city to visit because in 2027, it will host the International Building Exhibition, timed for the 100th birthday of the Weissenhofsiedlung, which pioneered the concept of modern living and is a UNESCO World Heritage site.

We invite you to join us for EuroVis 2027 in Stuttgart to enjoy talks, develop novel ideas, and strengthen the visualization community.
